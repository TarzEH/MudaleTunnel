# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for everyone, regardless of experience level, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, ethnicity, religion, or similar personal characteristic.

## Our standards

**Be respectful.** Disagreement is fine; disrespect is not.

**Be constructive.** Suggest improvements rather than just pointing out problems.

**Be patient.** Not everyone has the same experience level or first language.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainer at oriash94@gmail.com. All reports will be reviewed and investigated. The maintainer reserves the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
